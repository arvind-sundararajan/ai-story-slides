Introduction

OpenLIT was built by the openlit organization to solve AI observability.

OpenLIT is an open-source project that provides OpenTelemetry-native observability for large language models (LLMs) and AI development workflows. The project is built around the OpenTelemetry Collector, ClickHouse, and the OpenLIT SDK, which sends traces and metrics to the OpenTelemetry Collector for storage in ClickHouse. This architecture allows for efficient data collection and analysis, enabling developers to monitor and optimize their AI models. The OpenLIT UI pulls data from ClickHouse, providing a user-friendly interface for exploring and visualizing the collected data.

The specific problem that OpenLIT was built to solve is the lack of observability in AI development workflows, particularly when working with LLMs. As AI models become increasingly complex and widespread, it is essential to have tools that provide insights into their performance, latency, and other key metrics. OpenLIT addresses this need by providing a scalable and flexible observability platform that can handle the unique demands of AI workloads. By leveraging OpenTelemetry and ClickHouse, OpenLIT offers a robust and efficient solution for monitoring and analyzing AI models.

One of the key technical differences between OpenLIT and alternative solutions is its use of OpenTelemetry and ClickHouse. OpenTelemetry provides a standardized framework for collecting and exporting telemetry data, while ClickHouse offers a highly scalable and performant database for storing and analyzing large amounts of data. This combination enables OpenLIT to handle high-volume and high-velocity data streams, making it well-suited for AI workloads that generate vast amounts of data. Additionally, OpenLIT's architecture is designed to be modular and extensible, allowing developers to easily integrate it with other tools and platforms.

The target audience for OpenLIT is developers and data scientists working on AI and machine learning projects, particularly those using LLMs and other complex AI models. This includes researchers, engineers, and data analysts who need to monitor and optimize their models, as well as DevOps teams responsible for deploying and managing AI-powered applications. OpenLIT is also relevant to organizations that provide AI services and platforms, such as Google AI Studio, Together AI, and AWS Bedrock, as it can help them improve the performance and reliability of their offerings.
